# The Forsaken Light

## ⚠️ Content Warning

*The Forsaken Light* contains themes and situations that may be disturbing, emotionally intense, or difficult for some readers.

The story contains:

* **Verbal and physical abuse**
* **Bullying and harassment**
* **Depression and severe emotional distress**
* **Trauma and its psychological effects**
* **Family conflict and domestic abuse**
* **Betrayal and broken trust**
* **Psychological imprisonment**
* **Dissociation and altered states of consciousness**
* **Identity and internal conflict**
* **Substance-related themes**
* **Disturbing and emotionally distressing situations**

Some of these themes are central to the story.

**Reader discretion is advised.**

If you find yourself uncomfortable while reading, you are allowed to stop. You do not have to force yourself to continue.

## 📖 About

*The Forsaken Light* is a web-based story told across five chapters.

The story follows Taufik and Alter through experiences involving trauma, trust, betrayal, identity, and the psychological consequences of what they have been through.

## 🖥️ Recommended Experience

**The Forsaken Light is designed to be experienced on a desktop browser.**

While the story may technically be accessible from mobile devices, the website's visual layout, interactive elements, and multimedia content are designed with desktop in mind.

For the intended experience, reading on a computer and using chrome is strongly recommended.

Also, you need to be signed in to YouTube and Spotify website.

## 🎭 Themes

At its core, *The Forsaken Light* deals with:

* Pain
* Trauma
* Trust
* Betrayal
* Identity
* Isolation
* Emotional dependence
* The consequences of prolonged suffering

The story does not attempt to romanticize the experiences it depicts.

Some events are difficult precisely because they were difficult in reality.

## 🔒 Respect & Privacy

Because this story is based on real events, please treat the people and experiences involved with respect.

Do not use this project as a reason to identify, contact, investigate, harass, or otherwise interfere with real people who may be connected to the events described.

This project exists to tell a story about what happened—not to encourage harassment or retaliation.
